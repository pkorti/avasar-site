Precision induction

A patient-specific safe dose ceiling before anesthesia begins — replacing weight-based guesswork with a number built for that patient.

The problem

Induction dosing today is based almost entirely on body weight. It doesn't account for a patient's actual physiology — their baseline blood pressure, heart rate trends, or individual risk profile. For medium- and high-risk patients, crossing an unseen safety threshold can trigger hypotension or require a rescue intervention nearly half the time.

How it works

1

Pulls in existing patient data

Reads from the EHR and pre-op monitor history already being collected — no new hardware or workflow change required.

2

Builds a causal risk model

Uses causal inference methods, not simple correlation, to isolate what actually drives a safe dosing ceiling for that individual patient.

3

Surfaces a dose ceiling before induction

Delivers a single, clear number to the anesthesiologist before anesthesia begins — a ceiling, not a replacement for clinical judgment.

Who it's for

Built specifically for medium- and high-risk patients, where the cost of guessing is highest. Low-risk, healthy patients who easily tolerate standard induction are left alone — this isn't about adding friction everywhere, only where it matters.
