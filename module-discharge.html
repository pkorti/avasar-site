Smart discharge

Predicts PACU recovery time the moment a patient leaves the OR and flags likely extended stays early — easing bottlenecks and preventing premature discharge.

The problem

Discharge readiness from the post-anesthesia care unit is still judged largely with a decades-old scoring checklist, applied the same way regardless of the individual patient or procedure. The result cuts both ways: some patients are discharged before they're truly ready, and others sit in recovery far longer than needed — tying up beds and staff, and creating costly bottlenecks across the surgical schedule.

How it works

1

Captures the full intraoperative picture

Pulls together everything that happened during surgery — vitals trends, medications, procedure type, duration — the moment the case ends.

2

Predicts recovery time at OR exit

Produces an expected PACU duration immediately, before the patient has even been wheeled into recovery.

3

Flags likely extended stays early

Separately triages patients likely to need longer recovery, so staff and bed planning can adjust ahead of time rather than reactively.

Who it's for

Built for PACU and OR scheduling teams managing bed turnover and staffing, as much as for the clinicians deciding when a given patient is ready to go home.
